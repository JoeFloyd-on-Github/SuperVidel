Re: Interests in the CT60e still ?

  • Quote

Post by viking272 » Wed Sep 17, 2025 12:21 pm

It might be worthwhile understanding where they are with development and production of their latest version (SV3)? As I recall they were close 3-4 years ago to a much improved design, but life gets in the way and nothing further was announced.
Henrik and Torbjorn also told me the main fpga used on the SV2 dated from 2003 (Spartan?) and was discontinued in 2018, so another batch will be difficult.
The latest SV dev version was called SV3 and was based around a newer FPGA, Xilinx Zynq 7020 with Zturn and an adaptor interfacing with CT60. The VHDL code and adaptor board were mostly ready 4 years ago.
I think this info was on some postings a while ago.
